Selected settings   Reference: 30 kV, 2 mA

Illustrative curves, not measured or quantitatively predicted. Endpoint energy, cutoff wavelength and electron arrival rate are calculated. Current scaling is proportional at fixed voltage under the stated conditions. Heights across different voltages do not specify a physical output law.

Fixed target, filtration, geometry and exposure time. Steady voltage, negligible initial electron energy and small recoil neglected. Stable tube operation and linear detection. Characteristic lines omitted.

Equations and interpretation

Kmax = eV; Eγ,max = eV; λmin[nm] = 1.239841984 / V[kV]. Electron arrival rate = I/e. Curve height represents photon output per wavelength interval, not photon energy.

The cutoff is an energy bound. Most photons have smaller energies and longer wavelengths. At fixed V, increasing I does not increase eV.
