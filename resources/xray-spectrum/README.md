## Use this resource

1. Predict what changing voltage or current will do before moving a control.
2. Compare 20, 30 and 50 kV at fixed current. Record the cutoff and explain the energy bound.
3. At 30 kV, compare 2 and 4 mA. Explain which quantities change and which stay fixed.
4. In your notebook, explain why the broad spectrum maximum is not the maximum photon energy.
5. Return at the next class: calculate the cutoff at 35 kV independently, then check it here.

## Scientific scope

The calculated quantities are the endpoint energy, minimum wavelength and electron arrival rate. Spectral curves are illustrative, not measured or quantitatively predicted. Target, filtration, geometry and exposure time are held fixed; stable tube operation and linear detection are assumed. Initial electron energy and small recoil are neglected. Characteristic lines are omitted.
