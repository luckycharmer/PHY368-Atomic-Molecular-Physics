PHY368 Numerical Worksheet

After-class extension worksheet

Complete Questions 1–11 after the lecture. The separate in-class practice sheet contains the three tasks used in the ten-minute classroom activity.

Name: ______________________   Constants: hc = 1239.84 eV·nm; h = 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J·s; kB = 8.617×10⁻⁵ eV/K; c = 2.998×10⁸ m/s. Use E(Kα) ≈ 10.2 (Z−1)² eV unless told otherwise. Show units.

Part A. Moseley's law

1. Estimate the Kα photon energy (keV) and wavelength (nm) for zinc (Z = 30).

2. A characteristic line at 6.40 keV is assumed to be Kα. Estimate Z and name the element.

3. For the model E = 10.2 (Z−1)² eV: (a) write the slope and the horizontal and vertical intercepts of √ν against Z; (b) find a in Hz^½; (c) check √ν for copper two ways.

4. The tabulated Mo Kα₁ energy is 17.479 keV. Find the effective screening σ = Z − √(E/10.2 eV) for Mo (Z = 42). Compare with σ ≈ 1 and comment in one sentence.

Part B. Einstein coefficients

5. For g₁ = 3 and g₂ = 5, find B₁₂/B₂₁. Does this alone fix the ratio of total upward to downward transition rates?

6. For a transition at ν₀ = 5.0×10¹⁴ Hz find A₂₁/B₂₁ from the Einstein relation. State the radiation-density convention.

7. In thermal radiation B₂₁u/A₂₁ = 1/(eˣ−1). Evaluate it for (a) λ = 500 nm at T = 300 K and (b) λ = 3 cm at T = 300 K. Which emission process dominates in each case?

8. A two-level system has g₁ = 1, g₂ = 3, with N₁ = 1.0×10¹² and N₂ = 2.0×10¹². Is there a population inversion? Is there net absorption or net gain?

Part C. Radiative decay

9. An upper level has three radiative channels: 6×10⁷, 3×10⁷ and 1×10⁷ s⁻¹. Find τ, the branching fractions and the fraction remaining after 25 ns.

10. A level has one radiative channel, A = 2.5×10⁷ s⁻¹. How long until 90% of the population has decayed?

11. Explain in two sentences why B is called a coefficient while B·u is a rate, and why lifetime does not mean each atom waits τ before emitting.
