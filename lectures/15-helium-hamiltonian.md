# Lecture 15 — Helium Hamiltonian and Approximation Models

## Core focus
Two-electron Hamiltonian; electron–electron interaction; screening and approximation methods.

## Lecture outcomes
By the end of the lecture, students should be able to:
1. define the central quantities and physical model;
2. connect the model to measurable atomic or molecular spectra;
3. solve or interpret at least one quantitative or state-labeling problem.

## Reading
Foot; Bransden & Joachain

## Teaching structure
- 5–10 min prerequisite retrieval
- 30–40 min concept development
- 15–20 min derivation/worked example
- 10–15 min active problem or spectrum interpretation
- 5 min exit check
