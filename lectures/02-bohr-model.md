# Lecture 2 — Bohr Model of Hydrogen

## Central question
Can a simple quantitative atomic model explain the observed hydrogen spectrum?

## Learning outcomes
Students should be able to:

1. state the key assumptions of the Bohr model;
2. derive or use the allowed hydrogen energies;
3. calculate radii, ionization energies and transition energies;
4. identify where the Bohr model succeeds and where it fails.

## What the model explains
- hydrogen spectral wavelengths;
- hydrogen-like ions;
- an atomic energy scale;
- quantized bound-state energies.

## What it does not explain adequately
- transition intensities;
- selection rules;
- many-electron spectra;
- fine structure without additional corrections;
- the full wave nature of the electron.
