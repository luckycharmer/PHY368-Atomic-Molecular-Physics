# Lecture 3 — Moseley’s Law and Characteristic X-rays

## Central question
Can an element be identified from the frequency of its characteristic X-rays?

## Learning outcomes
Students should be able to:

1. distinguish bremsstrahlung from characteristic X-ray emission;
2. explain how inner-shell vacancies generate characteristic lines;
3. use Moseley-type scaling to estimate X-ray frequencies;
4. relate characteristic X-rays to nuclear charge and atomic number.

## Significance
Moseley’s measurements established atomic number (Z), rather than atomic mass, as the physically meaningful ordering variable for the periodic table.
