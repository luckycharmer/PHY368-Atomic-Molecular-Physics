# Books and Reading Guide

## Primary text
**C. J. Foot, _Atomic Physics_, Oxford University Press, 2005.**

Use as the principal anchor for hydrogen, angular momentum, Zeeman structure, fine structure, helium, many-electron atoms and angular-momentum coupling.

## Recommended texts

**W. Demtröder, _Atoms, Molecules and Photons_, 2nd ed., Springer, 2010.**  
Particularly useful for spectroscopy, radiative processes, transition probabilities, line shapes and excited states. For Lecture 4, Chapter 7, especially §7.1 and §7.1.1, directly treats transition probabilities and Einstein coefficients.

**B. H. Bransden and C. J. Joachain, _Physics of Atoms and Molecules_, 2nd ed., Pearson, 2008.**  
Useful for deeper atomic structure and most of the molecular-physics development, including bonding, LCAO, H₂ and molecular electronic states.

**A. P. Thorne, _Spectrophysics_, 2nd ed., Chapman & Hall, 1988.**  
Useful for line structure, transition intensities, selection rules and interpretation of atomic and molecular spectra.

## Reading principle
The course wiki maps each topic to the most suitable text rather than forcing one book to cover every lecture.
