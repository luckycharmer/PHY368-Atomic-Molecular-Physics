# Topic — Characteristic X-rays and Moseley’s Law

## Physical origin
Characteristic X-rays are produced when an inner-shell vacancy is filled by an electron from a higher shell.

They are distinct from the continuous bremsstrahlung background produced by electron deceleration.

## Interpretation
Moseley’s measurements provided direct experimental evidence that nuclear charge, represented by atomic number (Z), is the fundamental ordering quantity for the elements.

## Caution
The simplified hydrogenic model is valuable pedagogically but does not reproduce all experimental X-ray energies exactly. Accurate values require many-electron atomic structure.
